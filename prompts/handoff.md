# Hand a task to another model

Say this in the chat you want to hand off:

---

Hand this task off through my LifeGraph. Write a card to `handoff/<short-name>.md` with these sections, then link it from `handoff/handoff.md`:

- **Goal:** what we are trying to get done, in one or two lines.
- **Done so far:** decisions made and work finished, with file names or links.
- **Next step:** the single next action.
- **Open questions:** anything I still need to decide.
- **From:** your model name and today's date.
- **Status:** open

---

In the other app, say: "Read my LifeGraph and pick up the open handoff card." If that app has no connector, attach or paste the card file.
