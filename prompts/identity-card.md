# Identity card

For apps that cannot read your files (phone apps, or apps with no connector), paste a short card into their custom instructions or memory settings. Ask an assistant connected to your LifeGraph:

---

Read my LifeGraph (`index.md`, then `Me`, and the hubs). Write an identity card for me in under 600 words: who I am, what I am working on now, the people I mention most (first names only), and how I like answers. Use only `open` and `personal` files, never `private` ones. Plain text, short lines, no headings beyond one per section.

---

Paste the result into Claude (Settings, Profile), ChatGPT (Personalization, Custom instructions) or any other app. Regenerate it every month or so.
